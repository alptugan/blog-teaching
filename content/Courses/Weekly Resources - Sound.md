### week 03 - Diegesis & Listening Modes
1. Create a complete sound design for a short video, incorporating the diegetic, non-diegetic, and trans-diegetic layers of sound.  
   **Video Selection**: Select, shoot, or generate a video scene that is a maximum of 20-30 seconds long. The scene should provide an opportunity for both natural (diegetic) and stylized (non-diegetic/trans-diegetic) sound elements.  
   **Diegetic Sound**: Design a full and convincing soundscape for the scene using diegetic sounds (sounds that originate within the world of the video, like footsteps, dialogue, or environmental noises).  
   **Non-Diegetic or Trans-Diegetic Sound**: Incorporate at least one significant sound effect that is either non-diegetic (e.g., a musical score, stinger, or mood-setting sound not heard by the characters) or trans-diegetic (e.g., a sound that crosses between the world of the characters and the world of the audience, like a character's internal monologue or a highly stylized sound that represents a thought or memory).  
      
2. Export the video with sound in mp4 format. Keep the video resolution 1920x1080 maximum. [Tutorial](https://teaching.alptugan.com/Tutorials/Davinci-Resolve---Video-Export)  
      
3. Upload the videos [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1zGbmNFiOwqWqaqOAuv1aZkNKwRoulkB5?usp=sharing), and paste the link on LMS.  
      
4. Watch Fatih Akın's [Crossing The Bridge](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0459242/). Jot down diegetic, non-diegetic, and trans-diegetic scenes. Bring your notes to class. Do not submit.

1. Find at least 5 different objects to record their sounds. It can be anything e.g. a chain, metal forks, a vase, toys, clothes, constructional material, soundscape of an environment...
