## Part 3: Map Construction
- Plot your measurement points on a base map.
- Draw contour lines connecting equal dB values (e.g., 50, 60, 70).
- Use colour coding:
    - Blue/green = quieter zones
    - Orange/red = louder zones
- Annotate with sound sources (traffic, birds, voices, etc.).

---

## Part 4: Reflection Questions
Write 500–700 words addressing:
1. **Keynote sounds**: What constant background sounds define this space?
2. **Soundmarks**: Did you find any unique or culturally significant sounds?
3. **Spatial contrasts**: Where are the quiet refuges? Where are the noisy hotspots?
4. **Ecological/cultural implications**: What does this soundscape reveal about human activity, biodiversity, or social life in the area?
5. **Personal perception**: Did mapping change how you experience this place?

# 📝 Sample Worksheet: Isobel Map of Campus Courtyard

## Part 1: Preparation
- **Chosen site**: University courtyard (200m × 200m)
- **Boundaries**: Library entrance to cafeteria, including central fountain
- **Time of survey**: 11:00 AM
- **Weather conditions**: Sunny, light breeze

---

## Part 2: Fieldwork

|Point|dB Level|Sound Sources|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|48|Birdsong, fountain water|Quiet corner near trees|
|2|55|Student chatter|Benches near cafeteria|
|3|62|Traffic hum|Roadside edge of courtyard|
|4|70|Construction noise|Adjacent building site|
|5|50|Wind in trees|Shaded garden area|
|6|58|Footsteps, voices|Pathway crossing|

---

## Part 3: Map Construction (Conceptual Sketch)

- **Contours drawn at 50, 60, 70 dB**
- **Blue zone (≈50 dB)**: Garden and fountain area
- **Green zone (≈55–58 dB)**: Student seating areas
- **Orange zone (≈62 dB)**: Roadside edge
- **Red zone (≈70 dB)**: Construction site

## Part 4: Reflection (Excerpt)
- **Keynote sounds**: Constant traffic hum from the nearby road.
- **Soundmarks**: Fountain water and bird calls — unique to this courtyard.
- **Spatial contrasts**: The garden offers a quiet refuge (≈48–50 dB), while the construction site dominates with disruptive noise (≈70 dB).
- **Ecological/cultural implications**: The courtyard balances natural and social sounds, but construction threatens its acoustic identity.
- **Personal perception**: Mapping revealed how small shifts in location drastically change the soundscape — something unnoticed before.
