DAW is the acronym of *Digital Audio Workstation*. A DAW is a software built for audio related paradigms such as recording, editing and mixing multiple sound tracks. Sound designers & engineers, recording & [[Foley]] studios, sound artists, and many  other professionals work with sound utilize DAWs while working with sound. There are many paid and free software tools available in the  market for different purposes such as Cubase, Ableton Live, ProTools, Blackmagic Davinci Resolve, Reaper, FL Studio, Adobe Auditions, Logic Pro and BitWig, Wwise & FMod (Developed for game sound design)
