## 4. Add Markers
Play the video start to finish & note down the required sounds. Use <kbd>m</kbd> shortcut to add markers on your project.

![[04-Marker-optimized.jpeg|-center|500]]


## 5. Research for Audio Samples
Search or record the required sound effects relevant to your marker notes. You can refer to [[COD 323 Sound Design#Sound Sample Library|Sound Sample Library]] sources or record your own via your sound recorder.

You can add musical tracks  as well. Keep in mind to use royalty free sound tracks.

> [!TIP] Use key combinations to speed up your design process. Refer to the [[Key Shortcuts#Reaper|Key Shortcuts]].
