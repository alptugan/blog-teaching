Shift Registers are sequential logic circuits. They are used for storage or transfer of binary data. They can convert between serial and parallel data.

![[shift-registors.jpg|-center|500]]


**1. Serial In - Parallel Out (SIPO)**
A SIPO Shift Register (<font color="#ffc000">74HC595</font>) can expand number of microcontroller outputs.

**2. Parallel In - Serial Out (PISO)**
