![[davinchi_crossfade.png]]
- Enable the <font color="#00b050">Layered Audio Editing</font> and view the Layered Audio in the current audio track. Check the following [[Davinci Resolve - Layered Audio Editing]] on how to enable Layered Audio Editing.
- Position the second audio sample in the same track towards the one that you want to mix with cross-fade. 
- Drag the second track until you get the above image on the left.
- <font color="#00b050">Mouse over</font> one of the collapsing tracks, then drag the visible notch-like GUI element to the right or left. The inverted version of the curve will be created on the opposite track and it will create a linear cross-fade effect.
- You can disable the <font color="#00b050">Layered Audio Track</font> view from the `View` menu on top.
