The following tutorial showcases how to execute basic shape transformations using simple shapes. TinkerCAD may not seem to be an advanced 3D design tool. But the limit is your imagination. I highly encourage you to begin with such tools to push the boundaries of your creative practice. It also helps you to develop your way of seeing objects in abstract forms. Such abstraction level allow you to perceive how simple shapes create complex shapes.

**Creating Holes:** [Tinkercad How to cut a hole in a object ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r8L7HhxLMVU)

**Split Objects:** [Easiest Way To Cut An Object in Half Using TINKERCAD 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=04tjvDu54Yk)
