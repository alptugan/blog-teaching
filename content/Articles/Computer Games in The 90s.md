This essay presents my personal insights into Personal Computer (PC) games from 1990 to 2000. Although I wasn't a dedicated gamer in my youth, this decade represents the period when I primarily engaged with PC gaming. I have included a selection of games that, even if I didn't complete them, profoundly impressed me during that era for specific reasons. This analysis aims to explore the question of what makes these games so authentic that they remain vivid in my memory and for many other people. To address this, I will first briefly review the selected titles to provide necessary context, then analyze the possible reasons for their lasting impact, and finally, present my own inferences based on this analysis.
