## Background
Recently, the founder of [Netlify](https://medium.com/u/5250f9d9bd2f?source=post_page---user_mention--acc0dfeaec8d---------------------------------------) introduced a novel concept termed "Agent Experience" (AX), pertinent to the domain of Agent-based Artificial Intelligence ([[Agent Experience#^Biilmann2025|Biilmann, 2025]]). This encompasses the manner in which AI-driven agents engage with both physical environments and digital interfaces. For instance, specific agents are capable of navigating web pages to assist users in locating relevant content based on their inputs. The inquiry extends to how an AI agent interacts with a webpage or communicates with humans when making calls on behalf of individuals. Given the boundless nature of these questions, AX may hold the key to the future evolution of AI agents.
