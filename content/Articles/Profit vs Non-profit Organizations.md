## Tech Companies Have No Moat
In the leaked Google memo, it is declared that "We have no moat, and neither does OpenAI." The memo states that while Google and OpenAI have been focused on each other, open source projects have been solving major AI problems faster and more efficiently. 

The release of Meta's LLaMA model has enabled an explosion of open source innovation. The memo claimed that open-source AI models are outpacing Google and OpenAI. 

DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis does not believe open-source AI models will outpace Google. Hassabis states that Google will continue to lead the industry in the next decade. We cannot say that it is not going to happen, or vice versa. The AI industry, and the hype around it continuously evolving. At the same, time the hype is not just showing off emerging tools, the techical structure of LLM models make it possible that integrate companies enhance their products via AI technologies. For instance Notion, a note taking app, integrate AI to their product wisely. If you are a user, using the Notion to keep your knowledge base for a long time, it becomes always a problem searching, recalling previous notes or specific files. Sometimes one may loose lots of time while searching a specific paragraph, or data in a plethora of notes, collected, written through years. Notion's AI integration solves that problem. You just ask a question to the chat UI, and it finds contextually relevant information to the user. It even cite about, where AI bot, collects the data. So, you can review the note and confirm the answer is relevant or not. This also provides solution for AI hallucination issues as well. Because, the replies are collected from your own notes, and you can check the source about the relevancy. 

AI is similar to dot.com balloon, but this time, the tech is in a evolutionary manner and affects the tools that people use in their daily and vocational lives. 

On the other side, Deepseek R1, yet another LLM AI chat model, provides the nearly same quality to the users much cheaper than OpenAI's ChatGPT model. 

Google may think that they will shape the AI wars in the next decade, but the power of open-source communities should not be over estimated. Google may have 1000s of dedicated engineers, but nothing can be powerful than self-motivated people working on a project.
