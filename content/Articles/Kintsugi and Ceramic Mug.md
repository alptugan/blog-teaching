[[Kintsugi (金継ぎ)]] is a Japanese philosophical approach on repurposing or fixing broken objects instead of throwing them away. 

It's kind of opposite stance to capitalism. Fixing broken things are kind of passion for me. 

I had bought two cups from a local designer one for myself the other for my partner. 

What I like with this approach is I can dive deeper into every single detail of an object. In simplest sense, it is just a cup. But it inherits traces of the designer. Each object are unique. It is handcrafted. Of course I paid for it. 

I started with sanding broken parts and make them flat surfaces.
Since it is seramik material, you need to be sabırlı enough and take your steps as slow as possible. 

Then I prepared a simit shaped wooden part. Because the seramik handle parts were lost.

While preparing the wooden handle, there was a kind of inception happened. Because the wooden handle also broke away during cutting it to size. I spared plenty of time preparing this handle, so I said why not apply Kintsugi for the Kintsugi wooden part. 

I glued pieces. Clamp the parts using a plastic lastic for one day. I did not have rush. I took my time. Because this was not a project for myself. I think Kintsugi is a kind of ideological attitude on crafted objects independent than the agent who built it. In that sense taking care of a plant at home, or helping stray animals at street is not different than this approach. We help stray cats not because of it is a project for us. We help because we care for them. Maybe it is related to empathical view over the our surrounding.

Sometimes taking a trash from the street and putting it in a trash can is similar. You don't throw away the streets, or you don't discard the animals on streets. We try to fix things. It is good have such people around. Thank you all the people. You have for your the Kintsugi spirit that you are not aware :)
