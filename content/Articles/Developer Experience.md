## Background
When Apple released the iPhone as a new smart mobile phone product, it created a big leap in how people interact with technological tools. But the groundbreaking step was happened when Apple releases iPhone [[Glossary#^0bb03d|SDK]] publicly to the developers. By doing that the company transformed the product into a platform (Cohick, 2011). It caused to reveal a new type of user for the product. These users are third-party developers.

## What is DX?
DX which is short for Developer Experience is about facilitating a good experience for developers who build apps on a platform, in order to create a better overall user experience (UX) for the end users of the platform.
