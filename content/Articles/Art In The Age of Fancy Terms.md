The human actor surrounded by non-humans. Algorithms decide what to do, what to post, what content matters, what title becomes interesting to the viewer. In the end view, like, or comment count matters to call the human action. The action itself is the act of opting in a desire to view. This is not judging the non-human actually, because the non-human is the product of other humans. Actually, we are not surrounded by non-humans, we are surrounded by ductus of other humans. Non-human like social media is a kind of material object that we can interact physically. Actually, everything after the human's touch, becomes the digital representation that most humans cannot perceive. 

Such kind of interaction with the technology is the fastest and precise way of transforming behaviour into capital. When a human actor clicks on something, the clicked human becomes happy, maybe earn some cents as well. I don't have anyone around me earning money by being clicked. I can understand the way, unestablished artists tend behave like that, but do not understand others. This is not judging, just asking. Why? Why every single human being needs attention. Why we put the desire of increasing our happiness hormones too much? And, does it really work? I mean does it mean the numbers make the artwork valuable?
