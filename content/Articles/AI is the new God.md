## What's the environmental impact of AI?
src: [A Personalized Ecology of AI](https://mail.cyberneticforests.com/a-personalized-ecology-of-ai/)

### Speculative Values as Starting Point
There is no exact information about how much power AI does use. 
>Dr. Sasha Luccioni at Hugging Face. Here are some statistics:
>- Generating one AI image is "[equivalent to charging your cell phone](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16863.pdf?ref=mail.cyberneticforests.com)." (The amount of energy cost while charging a cell phone)
>- Creating 1,000 images with MidJourney would cost about 2.9 kWh at that rate.

>For comparison 100GB of backed-up data stored in a cloud service. One cloud user's power consumption can be between [60Kwh and 1600Kwh](https://toffeeshare.com/blog/15/How-much-energy-does-it-cost-to-store-data-online/?ref=mail.cyberneticforests.com#:~:text=It%20has%20been%20estimated%20that,KWh%20of%20energy%20per%20year.) in a year. In the best-case scenarios, leaving a bunch of music and photos on cloud storage is about as much energy as creating 20,000 images with MidJourney.

Traditional practices are not so innocent as well. A ceramicist firing an electric kiln for 5 hours could use [27 kWh](https://corp.paragonweb.com/faqs/how-do-you-figure-the-cost-of-firing-a-kiln/?ref=mail.cyberneticforests.com), or about 9,310 image generations.

### CO2 Emissions
Another concern is CO2 emissions. One image generated by a powerful AI model is roughly comparable to driving 21.6 feet in a car. According to the Hugging Face report, this figure rises significantly, stating that 1,000 images are equivalent to driving 4.1 miles.

As reported by the US Federal Highway Administration, the average male aged 35-54 drives 51 miles daily, totaling around 360 miles each week. Although I work from home, I drive about 36 miles to the office twice a week. The difference between these two commuting patterns amounts to roughly 72,000 images per week; if I maintained that, I would align with the typical American driver in my age group.


### Water Consume
AI image model can use [1.8 to 12 liters of water](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03271?ref=mail.cyberneticforests.com) per kWh, with a worst-case scenario of 35 liters of water for 1,000 images. This is much higher than traditional photography, where a gallon of water could develop about 72 images, using only 3.8 liters or 0.05 liters per image.

Eating a steak burger costs 54,000 images. The veggie version of the hamburger is not safe as well which is 46,771 images. A typical American citizen ears [2.4 burgers a day](https://qz.com/1171669/the-average-american-will-eat-the-equivalent-of-800-hamburgers-in-2018?ref=mail.cyberneticforests.com).

Let's assume I ate 3 burgers a week (I make the veggie version at home for lunch almost daily). That would be 140,313 AI images a week before my art making had an impact on my water consumption.
