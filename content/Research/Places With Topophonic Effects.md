The [[2026-05-15-Topophonic Effect|Topophonic Effect]] describes a phenomenon wherein natural formations or built environments, specifically curved historical remnants such as ruined masonry, function as passive [[Acoustic Mirror|acoustic mirrors]], causing disparate soundscape elements to converge and amplify at specific focal points.


The Topophonic Effect refers to the phenomenon in which natural formations or architectural structures, particularly curved historical remnants like ruined masonry, act as passive acoustic mirrors, concentrating and amplifying diverse elements of the soundscape at distinct focal points.
