**Binary data** is information represented using only two symbols: **0 and 1**. In computing, these correspond to electrical states (off/on, low/high voltage) in hardware. Every type of digital data—text, images, audio, programs—is ultimately stored and processed as sequences of binary digits [[Bit]]s. A group of 8 [[Bit]]s forms a [[Byte]], the basic unit of data in most computer systems.
