**PRD** is short for *Product Requirements Document*, which outlines the requirements and specifications for a product. It helps ensure that all stakeholders understand what the product should do. Recently, it is utilized for creating accurate [[Agent Skills]].

## Purpose of a PRD
A Product Requirements Document outlines the necessary features and specifications for a product. It serves to communicate what the product should do without detailing how it will be accomplished.

## Key Components of a PRD
- Feature Descriptions: Detailed explanations of what the product should include.
- Performance Metrics: Criteria to measure the product's success.
- User Needs: Insights into what customers expect from the product.

## Importance of a PRD
A well-structured PRD helps prevent misunderstandings between stakeholders and ensures that all necessary information is included. It is crucial for guiding the development process and avoiding delays.

In summary, PRD is essential in product development, particularly in software, to ensure clarity and alignment among all parties involved.
