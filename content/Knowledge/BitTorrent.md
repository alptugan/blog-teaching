The founder of BitTorrent is Bram Cohen. He developed the protocol in 2001 and released the first version of the BitTorrent client in 2002. The protocol quickly gained popularity due to its efficient design and ability to easily transfer large files over the Internet.(ref: 2)

BitTorrent is a peer-to-peer communications protocol for efficiently sending any type of file (ref1), large or small, over the Internet. It is used for sharing large amounts of data, such as music, movies, and software. It works by splitting up the data into smaller pieces and then distributing it across multiple computers. This allows users to download the data from multiple sources simultaneously, increasing the speed and reliability of the download. BitTorrent also supports encryption, allowing users to download files securely. There are many different BitTorrent clients available for different platforms, such as Windows, Mac, Linux, and Android.
