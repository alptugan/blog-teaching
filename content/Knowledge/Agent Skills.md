## Background
Skills are folders of instructions, scripts, and resources that an [[LLM]] loads dynamically to improve performance on specialized tasks. They are first introduced by the AI company [Claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512176-what-are-skills). Skills teach AI Model how to complete specific tasks in a repeatable way, whether that's creating documents with your company's brand guidelines, analyzing data using your organization's specific workflows, or automating personal tasks.


As of today (20.03.2026), you can find a complete walkthrough on how to use skills on VS Code in the following [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fabAI1OKKww) video.
