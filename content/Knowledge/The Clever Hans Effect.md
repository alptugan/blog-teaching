	<div style="flex: 2; min-width: 250px;">
	The <b>Clever Hans</b> effect refers to a psychological phenomenon where an animal (or human) appears to solve complex cognitive tasks, but is actually responding to <b>unconscious, non-verbal cues</b> given off by the person asking the question.
	<br><br>
	It demonstrates how easily human observers can accidentally influence an outcome without realizing it.
