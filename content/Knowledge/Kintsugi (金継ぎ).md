	<div style="flex: 2; min-width: 250px;">
	Japanese philosophical approach on repurposing or fixing broken objects instead of throwing them away. It is the art of repairing broken pottery using urushi lacquer mixed or dusted with precious metals like gold, silver, or platinum. It highlights the cracks instead of hiding them, treating the break and repair as part of the object’s history.
