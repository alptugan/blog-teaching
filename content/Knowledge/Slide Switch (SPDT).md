A small SPDT (Single Pole Double Throw) slide switch is a type of passive electronic component that allows one input to connect to one of two outputs, making it useful for controlling circuits with a single control point. It typically has <span style="color:#ffcc00;">three terminals</span>: one common terminal for input and two output terminals for switching between two different circuits.

They can be found in variety of electronic devices from house hold to industrial products. Basically, it is a mechanical component that physically enables or disables the current between wires. 

A light switch in your dormitory, iPhone lock/unlock button, or any device that has on and off switch.

## Types of SPDT Switches
SPDT switches come in various designs, including:

|Type|Description|Common Applications|
|---|---|---|
|Toggle Switch|Flipped up or down to switch between positions.|Light switches, simple circuits.|
|Pushbutton Switch|Activated by pressing a button, often momentary.|Control panels, alarms.|
|Slide Switch|Moves back and forth to select between positions.|Battery-powered devices, toys.|
|Rotary Switch|Uses a dial to select between multiple positions.|Radios, audio equipment.|

These switches are versatile and can be used in both manual and automated applications, making them essential components in many electronic devices.
