The <span style="color:#ffcc00;">Acoustic Niche Hypothesis</span> suggests that in a healthy, undisturbed ecosystem, non-human organisms (biophony) evolve to occupy specific "niches" in the soundscape. To avoid masking each other's signals, they vocalize at different frequencies or different times, much like radio stations occupying different spots on a dial to avoid interference.
